# Amazon Exclusive Overview – Power BI Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

**Amazon Exclusive Overview** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Amazon sales performance across product categories, customer regions, payment methods, and years.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key business metrics and helps identify revenue trends, profitability, product-category performance, regional contribution, and payment-method distribution.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales revenue, profit, orders, and quantity sold.
- Track revenue and profit trends over time.
- Compare revenue performance across product categories and customer regions.
- Understand revenue distribution across different payment methods.
- Compare yearly performance by product category.
- Provide an interactive dashboard for business performance monitoring.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI Desktop**
- **Power Query** – Data transformation and preparation
- **DAX** – Measures and calculations
- **Data Visualization** – Cards, line charts, stacked bar charts, donut charts, and combo charts

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Revenue Trend Over Time
A line chart tracks total revenue across the available years, making it easier to identify changes and overall revenue growth.

### 2. Profit Trend Over Time
The dashboard tracks profit performance over time to help evaluate business profitability alongside revenue.

### 3. Revenue by Product Category and Region
A stacked bar chart compares revenue across product categories such as Beauty, Books, Fashion, Home & Kitchen, Electronics, Sports, and related categories, segmented by customer region.

### 4. Revenue by Payment Method
A donut chart shows revenue distribution across payment methods including Wallet, UPI, Cash on Delivery, Credit Card, and Debit Card.

### 5. Revenue by Year, Category and Profit
A combination chart compares yearly category-level revenue with profit, providing a broader view of sales and profitability performance.

## 💡 Conclusion

The Amazon Exclusive Overview dashboard provides a centralized and interactive view of Amazon sales performance. It combines revenue, profit, order volume, quantity sold, customer regions, product categories, and payment methods into a single reporting interface.

This project demonstrates practical skills in **data visualization, data analysis, Power BI, Power Query, DAX, KPI reporting, and dashboard design**. The dashboard can help business users monitor performance, compare categories and regions, and make data-driven decisions.
